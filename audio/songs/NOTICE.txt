Instrumental arrangements of traditional public-domain melodies; no lyrics or existing commercial recordings are used. Performances assembled for this app from FluidR3 GM / MIDI.js Soundfonts samples (Benjamin Gleitzman and contributors), CC BY 3.0: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Samples were trimmed, faded, sequenced to the melodies and normalized. Melody titles/origins and note durations are documented in data/songs.json.
